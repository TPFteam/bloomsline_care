## The Bloomsline Story (Narrative Framework)

### The Problem (Relatable)
"Therapy is 1 hour a week. Life is the other 167 hours."

Most mental health progress happens (or doesn't) between sessions:
- Clients forget insights by the next session
- Practitioners can't see what happens in daily life
- Pattern blindness: people can't see what affects their mood
- Motivation myth: waiting to "feel ready" before taking action

### The Insight (Aha Moment)
"Real change isn't about big breakthroughs. It's about showing up, again and again."

Small, consistent moments of self-awareness compound into transformation. But people need:
1. A gentle way to capture these moments
2. Something that shows them their patterns
3. Support that's available 24/7, not just in sessions

### The Solution (Clear & Simple)
"Bloomsline bridges the gap between therapy sessions and daily life."

**For Members:** Capture moments, discover patterns, get AI support
**For Practitioners:** See your clients' journey, share resources, extend your impact

### Why Now?
- 68% adoption of tele-mental-health solutions post-COVID
- AI maturity enables meaningful companion experiences
- Medicare now reimburses digital mental health treatments (Jan 2025)
- Mental health destigmatization accelerating

## Pitch Deck Structure

### Email Deck (8-10 slides, self-explanatory)
Designed to be understood without presenter. More text, cleaner.

### Presentation Deck (10-12 slides, visual-heavy)
Designed for live pitch. Less text, more talking points.

**Recommendation:** Create ONE core deck that works for both, with a few extra "detail slides" in appendix for email sends.

## Design Principles

1. **Calm, not clinical** - Use the soft color palette (mint, coral, lavender)
2. **Human, not corporate** - Real language, not jargon
3. **Show, don't tell** - Product screenshots > feature lists
4. **Breathing room** - White space, not cramped slides
5. **One idea per slide** - Don't overwhelm

---

## Email vs. Presentation: Differences

| Aspect | Email Deck | Presentation Deck |
|--------|-----------|-------------------|
| Slides | 10-12 + appendix | 10-12 |
| Text | More explanatory | Minimal, talking points |
| Notes | Speaker notes included | No notes needed |
| Appendix | Yes (detailed) | No |
| File size | Optimized PDF | High-res |
| CTA | "Reply to schedule call" | "Questions?" |

## Objection Handling

**"How is this different from journaling apps?"**
→ Journaling is solo. Bloomsline connects to your care team. Plus AI that actually understands your patterns.

**"Why would practitioners adopt this?"**
→ They extend their impact without more hours. See what happens between sessions. Share resources easily.

**"What about privacy/HIPAA?"**
→ Built HIPAA-compliant from day one. Practitioners control what they see. Members own their data.

**"AI therapy is risky"**
→ Bloom is a companion, not a therapist. Supports reflection, doesn't diagnose or treat. Escalation pathways built in.

**"High churn in mental health apps"**
→ That's B2C. Our B2B2C model has accountability built in - practitioners keep members engaged.
