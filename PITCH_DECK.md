## A1: Product Features Detail

### For Members
- **Moments**: Photo, voice, video, text capture
- **Insights**: AI-powered pattern recognition

- **Balance**: Work/life/sleep tracking
- **Resources**: Access practitioner-shared materials

### For Practitioners

- **Resources**: Create, organize, share materials
- **Library**: 100+ pre-made clinical resources
- **Sessions**: Schedule and track appointments

---

## A2: Competitive Landscape

### Category 1: Wellness Apps (B2C)
**Headspace, Calm, Sanvello**
- Generic content, no practitioner connection
- High churn (download → use twice → delete)
- No personalized insights

### Category 2: Teletherapy (B2C)
**BetterHelp, Talkspace**
- Replaces practitioner relationship
- Expensive for ongoing use
- No between-session tools

### Category 3: Practice Management (B2B)
**SimplePractice, TherapyNotes, Jane**
- Billing, scheduling, documentation
- No client engagement features
- No between-session support

### Bloomsline: The Gap
- Enhances existing practitioner relationship
- Supports members between sessions
- Connects both sides of the care equation
