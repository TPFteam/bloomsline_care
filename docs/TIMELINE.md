## Key Decisions Along the Way

### Doctalink → Bloomsline Pivot (Q1 2025)
- **What was Doctalink?** A platform for practitioner profiles — helping therapists be found online
- **Why pivot?** Market was crowded (SimplePractice, Doctolib, Zocdoc) and timing was early
- **What we learned:** The real gap is between sessions, not in finding practitioners
- **Quote:** "We didn't just build a product. We earned the insight to build the right one."

### Solo Build (Q3-Dec 2025)
- Aditya built the entire platform solo
- Web dashboard: Next.js 16, Supabase, Tailwind, Framer Motion
- Mobile app: Expo/React Native, Supabase
- AI companion (Bloom): Always-available, context-aware

### Finding PMF (Q1 2026 — Current)
- Refining the product based on early user feedback
- Building the foundation across 4 pillars: Product (BCS), Digital Presence (PLG), Sales (CNE), UX (GUX)
- Preparing for seed round (€500K-€750K)
