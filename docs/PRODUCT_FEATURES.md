# Bloomsline — Product Features

## Member-Facing Features (Mobile App)

### Moments Capture
- Capture life moments in 10 seconds
- Three formats: photo, voice, or text
- No forced categorization — AI discovers patterns
- Connected to practitioner context
- Emphasis on self-expression, not data entry

### Bloom — AI Companion
- Always-available AI support
- Context-aware through BCS (Bloom Context System)
- Understands emotional patterns over time
- Safe space — connected to care, not replacing it
- Available between sessions when practitioner is not

### Seeds / Anchors
- Daily habits framed as "seeds" (botanical metaphor)
- Two types: **Grow** (build positive habits) and **Let Go** (release negative patterns)
- Daily check-in tracking
- Week view with dot matrix
- Month view with heatmap grid
- Growth section with streak tracking and detailed stats
- Activity history log
- No guilt for missing days (Gentle UX)

### Daily Rituals
- Adaptive to energy levels
- Connected to therapeutic goals
- Light-touch engagement — seconds, not minutes

### Progress Analytics
- Week strip: mood, rituals, anchors at a glance
- Mood tracking with emoji bubbles and distribution bars
- 30-day activity heatmap grid
- Expandable day panels
- Moments overview with type breakdown

## Practitioner-Facing Features (Web Dashboard)

### Member Visibility
- See member's week through what they share
- Engagement tracking over time
- Mood patterns and behavioral trends
- Session preparation context

### Worksheet Tools
- Create worksheets in one click
- Share directly with members
- Track completion and engagement

### Session Context
- Start sessions already informed
- See moments, moods, and patterns since last session
- Reduce catch-up time, go deeper faster

### Practice Management
- Member list with status tracking (active/inactive/pending)
- Session scheduling and management
- Progress notes and milestones
- Engagement level indicators

---

## Feature Philosophy

Every feature is evaluated through the Gentle UX lens:
1. Does it respect the user's emotional state?
2. Does it connect (not replace) the practitioner-member relationship?
3. Can it be completed in seconds, not minutes?
4. Does it avoid pressure mechanics (streaks, guilt, scores)?
5. Does it generate context for the BCS?
