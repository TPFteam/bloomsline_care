## Why Now — Market Tailwinds

1. **Post-2020 destigmatization:** Mental health conversations have gone mainstream since the pandemic
2. **AI companions are socially accepted:** People are comfortable using AI for emotional support (49% of those with mental health issues already use AI)
3. **Medicare covers digital mental health (Jan 2025):** Regulatory tailwind in the US market
4. **Insurance demands measurable progress:** Payers want data-driven outcomes, which Bloomsline enables through continuous engagement tracking

---

## Distribution Advantage

> Each practitioner brings 20-50 members. Built-in distribution, lower CAC than consumer apps.

Unlike B2C mental health apps that need to acquire each user individually, Bloomsline's B2B model means:
- 1 sale = 1 practitioner = 20-50 members activated
- CAC amortized across the practitioner's entire client base
- Network effects through the Care Network Effect (CNE)

## Competitive Landscape

### Category 1: Practice Management Tools
| Company | What They Do | Gap |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| **SimplePractice** | Full EHR — billing, scheduling, clinical notes, telehealth, client portal | No between-session engagement tools |
| **Jane App** | Clinic management — scheduling, charting, billing (popular in Canada) | Focused on operations, not care continuity |
| **TherapyNotes** | Clinical documentation, billing, telehealth | No member-facing experience |

### Category 2: Wellness / Consumer Apps
| Company | What They Do | Gap |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| **Headspace** | Meditation, mindfulness, sleep — consumer subscription | Generic content, no practitioner connection |
| **Calm** | Sleep, meditation, relaxation — consumer subscription | No therapeutic context or practitioner link |
| **BetterHelp** | Online therapy marketplace — connects users with therapists | Replaces the practitioner relationship, doesn't enhance it |

### The Gap Nobody Owns

```
Practice Tools ←——→ [GAP] ←——→ Wellness Apps
Run the business        ???        Help individuals cope
                         ↓
                    Bloomsline
          Between-session care platform
    Where both sides stay connected
```

**Key differentiator:** Bloomsline doesn't compete with either category. Practice tools run the business. Wellness apps help people cope. Bloomsline connects the practitioner and the client between sessions — a new category.
