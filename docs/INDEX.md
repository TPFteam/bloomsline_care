## Quick Reference

**One-liner:** Where small moments become meaningful change.

**The Problem:** Therapy is 1 hour a week. Life is the other 167.

**Four Pillars:**
- BCS (Bloom Context System) — The engine
- PLG (Practitioner-Led Growth) — How they find us
- CNE (Care Network Effect) — How we close
- GUX (Gentle UX) — Why they stay

**Business:** B2B SaaS, €29-79/mo per practitioner, members free

**Raising:** €500K-€750K seed, 18 months runway

**Status:** MVP complete, finding PMF (Q1 2026)
