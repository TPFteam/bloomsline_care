# Bloomsline — Branded Concepts

Four proprietary concepts that define how Bloomsline builds, grows, sells, and retains.

---

## BCS — Bloom Context System

**Pillar:** Product | **Why:** The engine

A behavioral context model that learns from every moment logged, understands emotional patterns over time, and gives practitioners and members a shared language for growth.

**Key points:**
- Not a chatbot. A context engine for human behavior.
- Learns from moments (photos, voice, text) logged by members
- Identifies emotional patterns and behavioral trends over time
- Creates a shared context between practitioner and member
- Enables practitioners to start sessions already informed

**Positioning:** While competitors build AI chatbots, BCS builds understanding. It's the intelligence layer that makes the entire platform smarter with every interaction.

---

## PLG — Practitioner-Led Growth

**Pillar:** Digital Presence | **Why:** How they find us

Every practitioner who joins publishes their story, their approach, and their expertise through Bloomsline. They become the content. They become the distribution.

**Key points:**
- Zero ad spend, organic flywheel
- Practitioners create SEO-optimized public pages by simply using the platform
- Each practitioner profile becomes a discoverable landing page
- Content is authentic (real practitioners, real approaches) — not generic marketing
- Natural distribution through professional networks

**Positioning:** Most B2B SaaS spends heavily on paid acquisition. PLG turns every user into a distribution channel. The more practitioners join, the more visible the platform becomes — with zero incremental marketing cost.

---

## CNE — Care Network Effect

**Pillar:** Sales | **Why:** How we close

Every practitioner brings 20-50 members. Those members see other practitioners. Those practitioners hear about Bloomsline through their clients. One sale seeds the next.

**Key points:**
- Growth compounds through care relationships that already exist
- 1 practitioner → 20-50 members → those members see other practitioners → those practitioners discover Bloomsline
- Built-in distribution mechanism, lower CAC than consumer apps
- Early adopter program turns first users into advocates
- The network effect strengthens with every new practitioner

**Positioning:** Unlike traditional SaaS where each sale is independent, CNE creates compounding growth. Each new practitioner doesn't just add revenue — they seed the next wave of practitioners through their clients' other care relationships.

---

## GUX — Gentle UX

**Pillar:** Design & Retention | **Why:** Why they stay

Most mental health apps copy fitness patterns: streaks, scores, guilt. 90% of users quit within a month. Gentle UX is our answer.

**Key points:**
- Progress shown as narrative, not numbers
- No pressure mechanics — no streaks, no guilt, no punitive design
- The interface itself is therapeutic
- Bilingual design (EN/FR) with cultural sensitivity
- "That's not design. That's retention."

**Positioning:** Competitors can copy features. They can't copy a philosophy baked into every interaction. GUX isn't a feature set — it's a design standard for emotional contexts that directly drives retention.
