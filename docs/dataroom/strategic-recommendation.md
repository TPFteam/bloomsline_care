## Current State Assessment

### Strengths
1. **B2B2C architecture is category-defining** (Decisive) — Only platform with practitioner tools + member app + AI companion in EU
2. **Projected unit economics look strong — on paper** (Projected) — LTV/CAC 72.5x projected, 85% gross margin, €50 CAC, 1.7-month payback. ALL projections — nothing measured.
3. **Product is built, not vaporware** (Strong) — Full MVP: Next.js 16 web + Expo mobile. 24+ API endpoints, 7 AI endpoints, 3-language i18n.
4. **EU regulatory positioning creates structural moat** (Strong) — GDPR-native, AES-256-GCM, RLS, EU AI Act-ready.
5. **Market timing is favorable — window is open** (Favorable) — BetterHelp -11%, Woebot shut down, Talkspace pivoting B2B. Zero AI-native practitioner SaaS in EU.

### Weaknesses
1. **Zero revenue. Zero paying customers. Zero validated demand.** (Critical) — 119 interviews + 15 beta testers = interest, not purchasing behavior.
2. **Two-person team is a single point of failure** (Critical) — No clinical advisor. Cannot match feature velocity of funded competitors.
3. **"Do nothing" is the real competitor** (High) — 80%+ of French practitioners use paper/nothing. Only 5% use AI.
4. **No clinical evidence base** (High) — No published outcomes, no trials. DiGA, EU AI Act, insurance all demand evidence.

## Strategic Options

### Option A: Capital Preservation (45% success probability)
- **Thesis:** Raise minimum viable, keep burn minimal, achieve PMF in one market before expanding.
- **Raise:** €250-300K | Burn: minimal | Founders €2K/mo each | No hire for 6 months
- **Focus:** France only, digital-only GTM
- **Runway:** 36+ months
- **Expected Outcomes:** M6: 25-40 practitioners, M12: 60-80 (€2-3K MRR), M18: 100-120 (€4-5K MRR)
- **Key Risks:** SimplePractice/Doctolib close window. Founders burn out. Cannot hire → product stalls.
- **Series A:** M24-M30

### Option B: Validated Expansion (55% success probability) — RECOMMENDED
- **Thesis:** 12-18 month market window is the strategic constraint. Prove PMF in France fast (90 days), then demonstrate multi-market viability.
- **Raise:** €400-500K | Burn: €10-12K/month | Hire engineer within 60 days
- **Focus:** France-first with 90-day milestone → Belgium/Switzerland M4-6 → Germany M8
- **Runway:** 28-33 months
- **Expected Outcomes:** M3: 15-20 practitioners, M6: 50-60 across FR/BE/CH (€2K MRR), M12: 120-150 (€5-6K MRR), M18: 200+ (€7-8K MRR)
- **Key Risks:** Multi-market stretches team thin. France PMF may take 6 months instead of 3.
- **Series A:** M16-M20

### Option C: Blitzscale Europe (35% success probability)
- **Thesis:** Speed is everything. Raise maximum, hire aggressively, 5 markets in 12 months.
- **Raise:** €500-750K | Burn: €15-18K/month | Hire engineer (M1), community manager (M3), clinical advisor (M3)
- **Focus:** Simultaneous FR/BE/CH → Germany/Spain by M8 → 5 live markets by M10
- **Runway:** 22-28 months
- **Expected Outcomes:** M3: 25-30 practitioners, M6: 80-100 (€3-4K MRR), M12: 200-250 across 5 markets (€8-10K MRR), M15: Series A
- **Key Risks:** Higher burn = less pivot room. Raising €500-750K pre-revenue in EU is hard. Speed may sacrifice quality.
- **Series A:** M12-M16

---

## Why Option B (Recommended)

1. **Balances speed and sustainability** — Fast enough for multi-market proof before window closes, sustainable enough for PMF discovery
2. **Validates before scaling** — 90-day France milestone is a hard gate. If fails, pivot before burning €400-500K
3. **Sufficient capital for execution** — Engineer hire removes bottleneck, 28-33 month runway with margin for delays
4. **Builds Series A narrative** — Multi-market traction (FR/BE/CH/DE) is compelling vs. single-market
5. **Mitigates key risks** — Engineer hire fixes R4, multi-market proves model isn't France-only
6. **Probability-weighted best outcome** — 55% success, €7-8K MRR + 200+ practitioners at M18

**Option A is too slow** — lets competitors close window. **Option C is too risky** — higher burn, less pivot room.
