## Executive Summary

The B2C therapy marketplace model is collapsing (BetterHelp -11% YoY). The B2B enterprise model is consolidating (Lyra $5.58B, Spring Health $3.3B). A new category — **AI-augmented practitioner SaaS with between-session care** — remains entirely unoccupied.

**Key signals:** $33B → $88B by 2030 | BetterHelp -11% Q1 | Talkspace +42% payor revenue | 0 EU competitors

## Macro Trends (5)

### MT1: Mental Health Demand Explosion (Impact: 9/10)
- **Force:** Demand-side structural shift
- **Timeline:** Now → 5+ years
- Key stats: 1B+ globally affected (WHO 2025), 86% untreated, 58% providers report longer waitlists, 42% increase in anxiety/depression post-2020
- **So what:** Bloomsline doesn't need to create demand — it's being pushed toward practitioners by a supply-demand gap. More demand = more sessions = more between-session hours where Bloomsline adds value.

### MT2: AI-Augmented Care Goes Mainstream (Impact: 9/10)
- **Force:** Technology-side enabler
- **Timeline:** Now → 3 years
- Key stats: 49% of MH patients use AI (Sentio 2025), 42% of clinicians use AI for admin (APA), AI scribes save 12-15 hrs/month, Market: $3.3B → $17.3B by 2030
- **So what:** Practitioner AI adoption is accelerating from early adopter to early majority. The window to be the "AI-native practitioner platform" in the EU is 12-18 months before incumbents (SimplePractice, Doctolib) add AI features.

### MT3: Practitioner Burnout Crisis (Impact: 8/10)
- **Force:** Supply-side constraint
- **Timeline:** Now → 3 years
- Key stats: 93% of behavioral health workers report burnout, 29% considering leaving, 73% more paperwork than 5 years ago, average 12-15 hrs/week on documentation
- **So what:** Burnout = urgent purchase motivation. Practitioners don't buy software for "nice to have" — they buy when drowning. AI notes and between-session automation directly address burnout.

### MT4: B2C Therapy Marketplace Collapse (Impact: 8/10)
- **Force:** Competitive landscape restructuring
- **Timeline:** Now → 2 years
- Key stats: BetterHelp -11% YoY revenue, Woebot shut down June 2025 ($123M raised), Talkspace pivoting to B2B, consumer willingness to pay declining
- **So what:** B2C is dying. B2B2C is the replacement model. BetterHelp's collapse validates Bloomsline's architecture — practitioners as distribution, not direct-to-consumer.

### MT5: EU Regulatory Infrastructure as Competitive Moat (Impact: 7/10)
- **Force:** Regulatory environment
- **Timeline:** 1-3 years
- Key stats: EU AI Act (Aug 2026), EHDS (2027-2031), GDPR €4.5B+ cumulative fines, France MonParcoursPsy expanding TAM
- **So what:** EU regulation is a feature, not a bug. US competitors face 12-18 months of compliance work to enter EU. GDPR-native, EU AI Act-ready positioning creates a structural moat that no acquisition can bypass.
