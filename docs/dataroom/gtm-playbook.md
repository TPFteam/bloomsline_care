## 2. Channel Strategy (Ranked by ROI)

### #1 Founder-Led LinkedIn Outreach (ROI: 9/10)
- **Cost:** €2-5/lead (time only) | **When:** Pre-launch → M6
- **Why:** Highest ROI at pre-seed. 287% higher reply rate for multi-channel. Zero cost.
- **Playbook:** Build 200-target list → Connection request → Value post → DM with case study → Demo offer → Follow up 3x min
- **Targets:** Reply rate > 15%, demo rate > 5%, close rate > 20%

### #2 Referral Program (ROI: 9/10)
- **Cost:** €19-29 (1 month free) | **When:** Activate at 10 users → ongoing
- **Why:** 86% of B2B buyers say WOM most influential. Referred customers 16-25% higher LTV.
- **Playbook:** 1 free month per referral (both sides) → Ask at day 14 → Dashboard button always visible → Cap 3 free months/year
- **Target:** 20-30% of signups by M12

### #3 French Blog + SEO (ROI: 8/10)
- **Cost:** €5-15/lead (time) | **When:** Start M1 → ongoing
- **Why:** Low-competition French keywords. Compounds over time. 30% of pipeline by M6.
- **Playbook:** 1 post/week in French → Keywords: "gestion cabinet", "suivi patient entre séances", "RGPD psychologue" → CTA: free trial → Repurpose: carousel + newsletter
- **Target:** 1,000 organic visits/mo by M6, 5,000 by M12

### #4 Events & Conferences (ROI: 7/10)
- **Cost:** €30-80/lead | **When:** M3 → M18
- **Playbook:** AFTCC workshops, FFPP/SNP chapters, Congrès Français de Psychiatrie → 20+ contacts per event → 48-hour follow-up

### #5 Training Institute Partnerships (ROI: 7/10)
- **Cost:** €0 (free accounts) | **When:** M6 → M18
- **Playbook:** AFTCC, IFFORTHECC, IRCCADE, Asadis → Free year for newly certified → Co-branded toolkit → 3 partnerships by M12

### #6 Podcast Guest Appearances (ROI: 6/10)
- **Cost:** €0 | **When:** M6 → M18
- **Playbook:** Deux Psys, Catherine la Psy, Le Comptoir → "What happens between sessions" angle → 3 appearances in 12 months

### #7 Email Newsletter (ROI: 6/10)
- **Cost:** €1-3/lead | **When:** M3 → ongoing
- **Playbook:** Bi-weekly: 1 insight + 1 tip + 1 CTA → A/B subject lines → 500 subs by M6, 2,000 by M12 → Open >35%, CTR >5%

## 3. Messaging Framework

**Core Value Prop:** Bloomsline fills the 167 hours between sessions — so practitioners see their clients' week, and members feel supported every day.

**Positioning:** A platform that connects practitioners and members through gentle, AI-assisted between-session support — purpose-built for the EU mental health market.

### Taglines
1. "Therapy is 1 hour a week. Life is the other 167."
2. "See their week. Support their journey."
3. "Between-session care that practitioners trust and members love."

### Supporting Messages by Audience

| Audience | Message | Proof Points |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| Practitioners | Start every session already informed | 50% of time catching up (APA), real-time dashboard, AI session briefs |
| Members | Your growth continues between appointments | Bloom AI 24/7, 10-second moments, AI-discovered patterns |
| Investors | One sale. 20-50 users. Zero incremental CAC. | Projected LTV/CAC 72x, 90% margin, 1.7-month payback (all modeled) |

### Objection Counters
- "No time for a new tool" → White-glove setup in 15 minutes. No data migration.
- "I already use Doctolib" → We complement, not replace. Booking vs. care.
- "€19-29/mo is too much" → Less than one cancelled session (€60-80). Doctolib is €139.
- "Data privacy concerns" → EU-hosted, GDPR-native, AES-256, RLS on every table.

## 4. Content Strategy (by Funnel Stage)

### Awareness
- LinkedIn thought leadership (3x/week)
- French SEO blog posts (1x/week, 1,500+ words)
- Podcast guest spots (1x/month from M6)
- Conference presentations (quarterly)

### Consideration
- Practitioner case studies (written + 90-sec video, 2x/month)
- Comparison guides vs. SimplePractice, Quenza, Doctolib (evergreen)
- Email nurture sequence (5-email drip)
- Live demo recordings (monthly update)

### Decision
- ROI calculator (interactive, evergreen)
- Pricing page with social proof (3-tier + testimonials)
- Free trial (14 days, 60 for events, no credit card)
- Personal demo call with founder (15 min)

### Retention & Advocacy
- Onboarding email sequence (7 emails over 14 days)
- In-app engagement nudges (tooltips, milestones)
- Monthly practitioner digest ("47 moments logged this month")
- Referral prompts (in-app + email at day 14)
