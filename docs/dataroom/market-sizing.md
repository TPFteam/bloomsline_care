## European Tailwinds

1. **France:** Reimburses 12 psychology sessions/year — no GP referral required (MonParcoursPsy). Mental health is France's "Grande Cause Nationale" 2025. 5,500+ partner psychologists enrolled.
2. **EU Budget:** €1.23B committed to mental health — 20 flagship initiatives. First time EU treats mental health as cross-policy priority.
3. **Germany DiGA:** 1M+ prescriptions for digital health apps — mental health is #1 category. World's first national insurance-reimbursement pathway. 45% target mental health.
4. **GDPR + EHDS:** Structural trust advantage for EU-native health tech. In mental health, GDPR compliance is a product differentiator.
5. **Workforce shortage:** Europe has only 9.7 psychiatrists per 100K people — workforce is shrinking. Tools that multiply practitioner capacity address a system-level crisis.
6. **Economic cost:** Mental health costs Europe €600B+ annually — 4% of EU GDP. Productivity losses (€240–260B) exceed direct healthcare spending (€190B).

## Global Tailwinds

1. Post-2020 destigmatization — teletherapy is permanent. Market nearly triples by 2034.
2. 1 billion people live with a mental health condition — 91% with depression can't access care. Only 2% of health budgets go to mental health.
3. Mental health is #1 global health concern for employees — $438B in global productivity losses (Ipsos 2024).
4. 49% of people with MH issues already use AI for support — consumer comfort growing faster than regulation.
5. Insurance payers demand measurable outcomes — drives adoption of tracking tools.
