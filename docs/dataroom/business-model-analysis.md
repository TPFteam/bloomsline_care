## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Bloomsline is building the between-session care layer for mental health — a dual-sided platform connecting practitioners (B2B SaaS, €19-49/mo) and their clients (free companion app) in the 167 hours between therapy sessions where no tool currently exists.

**The investment thesis in four lines:**

1. €12B EU digital mental health market growing at 25% CAGR with zero dominant between-session platform
2. B2B2C model where each practitioner brings 20-50 members at zero CAC — organic distribution machine
3. 83% gross margin, €50 blended CAC, 5x LTV/CAC, 2-month payback — SaaS-grade unit economics (modeled)
4. Raising €400-500K pre-seed for 18 months to prove PMF → 100+ practitioners, 80%+ retention, Series A ready

**Critical caveat:** Every financial metric in this memo is modeled, not observed. Zero paying customers today. The quality of the model is high; the proof is zero. This analysis distinguishes clearly between validated data (interviews, market research) and assumptions (pricing, churn, adoption).

## 1. CORE VALUE PROPOSITION

### The Problem

Therapy happens in a 50-minute session. Life happens in the 167 hours between sessions. Today, those hours are a black box.

| Dimension | Data Point | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Session inefficiency | ~50% of session time spent catching up | APA Practice Guidelines 2024 |
| Treatment gap | 86% of people with MH conditions receive no treatment | WHO World Mental Health Report 2025 |
| AI readiness | 49% of people with MH issues already use AI tools | Sentio Research 2025 |
| Homework non-completion | 20-50% of therapy homework goes uncompleted | Clinical literature meta-analysis |
| Practitioner admin burden | 5-8 hours/week on notes, scheduling, follow-ups | Bloomsline discovery interviews (n=119) |

### Why This Problem Is Urgent and Costly

**For practitioners:**
- 5-8 hours/week on admin = €10,400-€16,640/year in lost clinical time (at €40/hr)
- 2-3 client dropoffs/year from disengagement = €1,600-€2,400 lost revenue per practitioner
- Zero visibility into what happens between sessions = sessions start from scratch every week

**For clients:**
- Therapy insights forgotten by day 3
- No way to share progress moments with therapist
- Feel alone between sessions → disengage → drop out

**For the system:**
- 86% treatment gap persists because care doesn't extend beyond the session
- €600B+ global economic burden of mental health conditions (WHO)

### Quantified Value Creation

| Value Driver | Annual Value to Practitioner | Bloomsline Cost | Value Multiple |
|---|---|---|---|
| Admin time saved (5-8 hrs/week × €40/hr) | €10,400-€16,640 | €348/yr | 35x |
| Client retention improvement (2-3 fewer dropoffs) | €1,600-€2,400 | €348/yr | 5x |
| AI-assisted notes (20min → 5min per session) | €7,800+ | €348/yr | 26x |

| **Total annual value delivered** | **€23,300+** | **€348/yr** | **~67x** |

Bloomsline captures 1.5% of the value it creates. This is deliberate — underpriced for adoption velocity at this stage.

### Competitive Differentiation

**Why now:**
- Post-2020 destigmatization increased demand 40%
- AI companions socially accepted (49% already use AI for MH)
- France declared mental health "Grande Cause Nationale" 2025
- Mon Soutien Psychologique (govt reimbursement) creating practitioner demand for tools
- No dominant between-session platform exists in EU

**Why us:**
- 119 discovery interviews across 7 countries shaped every feature
- Full product built by founders (zero outsourcing, zero pre-launch burn)
- Only platform connecting both sides of care (practitioners + clients) in one system
- European-native: GDPR-first, French-first, EU hosting

**The market gap:**

| Category | Players | What They Do | What They Don't Do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Practice Tools | SimplePractice, Jane, Doctolib | Run the business (billing, scheduling, EHR) | No member engagement, no between-session care |

Woebot's shutdown ($123M raised, FDA Breakthrough Device, Stanford-backed) validates that standalone AI mental health doesn't work. Care must be anchored in real therapeutic relationships.

### Pricing Architecture

**Tier 1 — Essentiel: €19/month** (€190/yr annual)
- Target: Solo practitioners testing the platform
- Up to 10 active clients, basic Bloom AI, manual session notes
- Designed to feel limited → drives upgrades within 60 days
- Gross margin: ~78%

**Tier 2 — Pro: €29/month** (€290/yr annual) — *Recommended / "Most Popular"*
- Target: Independent practitioners with 15-25 active clients (core ICP)
- Unlimited clients, full Bloom AI, AI-assisted notes, pattern detection, custom resources
- €29 is charm price below €30 psychological barrier, less than 1 cancelled session (€60-80)
- Gross margin: ~85%

**Tier 3 — Cabinet: €49/month base + €19/practitioner** (€490/yr + €190/head)
- Target: Group practice directors (3-8 practitioners)
- Multi-practitioner management, team dashboard, cross-practitioner insights, API access
- 5-practitioner group = €144/mo (~€29/head) — volume discount baked in
- Gross margin: ~87%

**Member Premium: €3/month** (opt-in)
- Advanced AI, extra rituals, priority pattern detection
- Conversion target: 5-8% of members
- vs Headspace €12.99/mo: 77% cheaper

### Pricing Logic

**Value-based, not cost-based.** At €4.25/mo variable cost, even the entry tier (€19) delivers 78% gross margin. Pricing is set by willingness-to-pay research:

| Price Point | Demand Index | Revenue Index | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| €15 | 100 | 60 | Maximum adoption, unsustainable |
| €19 | 95 | 72 | High adoption, charm price |
| €25 | 85 | 85 | Sweet spot near blended ARPU |
| **€29** | **78** | **91** | **Near-optimal — recommended** |
| €39 | 65 | 101 | Revenue max, filters tire-kickers |
| €49 | 50 | 98 | Premium signal, group practices |
| €79 | 30 | 95 | Requires proven outcomes |
| €99 | 18 | 71 | Too expensive for solo French practitioners |

**Key insight:** €10/mo ARPU difference (€19 vs €29) = 3x revenue difference at M18. Getting 70% of practitioners onto Pro (€29) vs Essentiel (€19) is the single most important pricing lever.

### Major Cost Drivers

2. **CAC spend** (~15-20% at scale) — Entirely organic; if paid acquisition needed, this jumps materially

### Operating Leverage

The model has strong operating leverage characteristics:

- **Variable costs scale at €4.25/practitioner** — fixed at any scale
- **Fixed costs step up slowly** (€2,500 every 6 months)
- **Contribution margin is 78.4%** — every incremental practitioner above break-even drops ~€23 to bottom line
- **At 850 practitioners (M36):** €24,650 MRR on ~€21,755 total costs = operating profitability

### Capital Intensity: LOW

- No physical infrastructure
- No inventory
- No hardware
- Minimal CAPEX (cloud-native)
- Primary capital need: team salaries + time to reach PMF

**Phase 2 (M12-M24):** Behavioral pattern dataset — unique, consent-based data on between-session mental health behaviors

The data asset created by daily member engagement (moments, moods, patterns) is potentially more valuable than the SaaS revenue. No one else is collecting this data in the context of real therapeutic relationships.

### Ecosystem Play

At sufficient scale, Bloomsline becomes a two-sided marketplace:
- **Supply side:** Practitioners create and share therapeutic resources
- **Demand side:** Members consume resources and provide engagement data
- **Platform take:** Resource marketplace commission + data insights revenue
- **Network effects:** More practitioners = more resources = more member engagement = more practitioners

This is the long-term play. At pre-seed, it's a vision, not a plan.
