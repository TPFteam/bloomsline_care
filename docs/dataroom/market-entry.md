## Entry Modes

### 1. Digital-First Entry (Recommended for Phase 2-3)
- Cost: €5-15K per market | Timeline: 4-8 weeks
- Pros: Lowest cost, fastest launch, fully reversible, tests demand before commitment
- Cons: Limited local knowledge, harder trust-building, support timezone gaps

### 2. Direct Entry
- Cost: €80-150K per market | Timeline: 6-12 months
- For: Germany (DiGA requires local entity) or US entry
- Pros: Full control, deep market understanding
- Cons: Highest cost, slowest, fixed overhead

### 3. Partnership / Joint Venture (Recommended for training institutes)
- Cost: €10-30K setup + 20-30% revenue share | Timeline: 3-6 months
- Pros: Instant local credibility, access to practitioner networks, near-zero CAC
- Cons: Revenue share reduces margin, partner dependency

### 4. Acquisition
- Cost: €200K-2M+ | Timeline: 4-8 months
- For: Post-Series A in competitive markets (UK, US)
- Pros: Instant user base + revenue, local compliance

### 5. Licensing / Franchise
- Cost: €15-25K legal setup per market | Timeline: 6-9 months
- For: Distant markets outside EU/NA (MENA, Asia) — post-Series B only

## Localization Requirements

### Belgium (Low Effort)
- **Product:** No UI changes — fr-BE via i18n. Belgian insurance integrations (INAMI/RIZIV).
- **Pricing:** €19-29/mo (same as France)
- **Legal:** GDPR applies directly. No HDS-equivalent.
- **Talent:** No local hire initially. French-speaking community manager (part-time) by M6.

### Switzerland (Low Effort)
- **Product:** No UI changes for Romandie. CHF currency support important.
- **Pricing:** CHF 29-39/mo (premium — Swiss earn €80-120K, 1.5-2x France)
- **Legal:** Non-EU but nDSG GDPR-equivalent. No EU AI Act obligation.
- **Talent:** No local hire for Romandie. FSP partnership within 3 months.

### Germany (High Effort)
- **Product:** Full German localization (UI, AI prompts, member content, help). DiGA-compatible data export (FHIR).
- **Pricing:** €25-35/mo. DiGA reimbursement: €200-500 per 90-day RX if approved.
- **Legal:** GDPR + BDSG (strictest EU). DiGA requires BfArM approval.
- **Talent:** German country manager (€50-65K), clinical advisor, DiGA consultant (€15-25K), content writer.

### Spain (Medium Effort)
- **Product:** Spanish localization exists (es) — verify clinical terminology.
- **Pricing:** €15-22/mo (Spanish practitioners earn €35-50K)
- **Legal:** GDPR + LOPDGDD. AEPD active enforcement.
- **Talent:** Spanish community manager (part-time, €25-35K).

### United Kingdom (Medium Effort)
- **Product:** English UI exists — adapt for British English. NHS DTAC compliance.
- **Pricing:** £22-29/mo
- **Legal:** UK GDPR (post-Brexit). CQC registration may be required.
- **Talent:** UK country manager (£45-55K), clinical lead with HCPC registration.

### United States (Highest Effort)
- **Product:** Full HIPAA compliance (BAA, encryption, audit logging). EHR integration (HL7 FHIR).
- **Pricing:** $39-59/mo
- **Legal:** HIPAA + HITECH, state licensure laws vary.
- **Talent:** US country manager ($60-75K), clinical lead, billing/compliance specialist.
