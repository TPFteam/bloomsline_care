## Thesis: Practitioner-Led Growth

We don't sell to consumers. We sell to practitioners (€19/29/49 per month) — they invite their clients. Our hypothesis (unproven): one practitioner sale = 20-50 member accounts at no extra cost. If this holds, each practitioner who joins could seed the next wave through peer word-of-mouth. We have 15 beta testers and 119 discovery interviews — no paying customers yet.

## Channel Strategy — Ranked by Priority

### 1. Direct Outreach (LinkedIn + Email) — Primary, Now
- **When:** Pre-raise → M6
- **Why:** Highest leverage at early stage. Multi-channel gets 287% higher reply rate vs single channel. At €19-29/mo, you need volume: 50+ conversations/week.
- **How:** Build list from LinkedIn Sales Navigator + Doctolib. Personalize every message. Offer 15-min demo, not a sales pitch. Follow up 3x.

### 2. Content Marketing (French blog + SEO) — Start now, compounds
- **When:** M1 → ongoing
- **Why:** French practitioners search for "gestion cabinet", "suivi patient", "RGPD psychologue". Low competition in French. SEO compounds over time.
- **How:** Publish weekly in French. Target keywords practitioners search. Share on LinkedIn. Repurpose for social. Goal: organic inbound by M6.

### 3. Events & Conferences — High, credibility
- **When:** M3 → M18
- **Why:** SimplePractice founder got first customers by attending local chapter meetings. AFTCC has 2,500 members. Congrès Français de Psychiatrie is the annual gathering.
- **How:** Attend AFTCC workshops. Present at FFPP/SNP events. Demo at Congrès (Dec 2025 Cannes, Dec 2026 Strasbourg).

### 4. Referral Program — Activate at 10 users
- **When:** M3 → ongoing
- **Why:** 86% of B2B buyers say word-of-mouth is most influential. Referred customers have 16-25% higher LTV and lower churn.
- **How:** 1 free month per successful referral. Ask every user for 2 introductions. Built into dashboard: "Invite a colleague" button.

### 5. Training Institute Partnerships — Medium-term, high leverage
- **When:** M6 → M18
- **Why:** 21% growth in psychologist numbers = thousands of new practitioners setting up practice each year. Easiest early adopters.
- **How:** Partner with AFTCC, IFFORTHECC, IRCCADE, Asadis. Free year for students/newly certified. "Recommended tool" positioning.

### 6. Podcast Guest Appearances — Complement, authority
- **When:** M6 → M18
- **Why:** Practitioners listen to Deux Psys, Catherine la Psy, Le Comptoir de la Psychologie. Builds authority and trust with zero cost.
- **How:** Pitch guest spots on 3-5 French psych podcasts. Share practitioner stories and between-session care insights.

## Key Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|------------|
| Slow adoption / long sales cycles | High | Multi-channel outreach, free trial, white-glove onboarding, low entry at €19/mo Essentiel tier |
| Practitioners don't see enough value | Medium | Focus on member engagement data — show practitioners their clients are using it |
| Doctolib adds engagement features | Low-Med | Move fast. Our member-facing app + AI between-session support is structurally hard to bolt on as an afterthought |
| HDS compliance required | Medium | Get legal advice early. Evaluate HDS-certified hosting (Scalingo, OVHcloud) as backup |
| Runway runs out before traction | Medium | Keep burn under €10K/mo. Milestone-based spending. Don't hire until PMF signal. |

## The Bottom Line

We're not building a marketing machine — we're building practitioner-led distribution. Our hypothesis: each practitioner who joins invites 20-50 members. Those members see other practitioners. Those practitioners hear about us. This is unproven but directionally supported by 119 discovery interviews. With €400K-500K, 2 founders (+ 2 planned hires), and 18 months of runway, we target 280 practitioners and €84K ARR — enough for a credible Seed round conversation.
