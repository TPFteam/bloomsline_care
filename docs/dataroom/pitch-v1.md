## Slide 2: Problem
**There are 168 hours in a week. Therapy is 1. What happens in the other 167?**

That's where they need support — and where progress gets lost.

| Stat | Source |
|------|--------|
| ~50% of session time spent "catching up" | APA Practice |
| 86% receive no treatment globally | WHO Sept 2025 |
| 58% of providers: waitlist longer than ever | National Council 2024 |
| 49% with mental health issues use AI | Sentio 2025 |

---

## Slide 3: Solution
**Bloomsline fills the 167.**

A companion app for members. A visibility tool for practitioners.

### For Members (Companion App)
- Log moments in 10 seconds (photo, voice, or text)
- See AI-discovered patterns in mood and behavior
- Chat with Bloom — an always-available AI companion
- **Outcome:** They stay engaged between sessions.

### For Practitioners (Visibility Dashboard)
- Understand their week through what they share
- Share worksheets and resources in one click
- Start sessions already informed
- **Outcome:** Sessions go deeper, faster.

*One platform. Two sides of care. Connected.*

---

## Slide 4: Why Bloomsline

### Why Practitioners Choose Us
- Clients regress between sessions
- No way to see the full picture
- Limited tools beyond the couch
- Burnout from emotional labor
- **Value:** Clients stay longer. Sessions go deeper.

### Why Members Stay
- Apps feel like work
- Tracking but not changing
- Alone between sessions
- Guilt when they slip
- **Value:** Real progress. Not just numbers.

## Slide 5: Product
**Built for both sides**

Member features: Moments (mood tracking), Rituals (adaptive daily practices), Progress (narrative growth), Bloom AI (personalized guidance)

Practitioner features: Resources (worksheets, exercises), Sessions (scheduling), Overview (engagement insights)

*"See not just hard days, but the full picture — the effort, the small wins, the patterns over time."*

## Slide 6: Core Features

### Moments (For Members)
- Capture everyday life — a walk, a meal, a feeling
- Snap a photo. Add how you felt. Done.
- 10 seconds. No streaks. No guilt if you skip.
- **Reward:** Scroll back and see how far you've come.

### Resources (For Practitioners)
- Create great materials and share with one click
- Build a library — worksheets, exercises, guides
- Your toolkit grows over time. Less repetition, more impact.
- **Reward:** Create once. Help hundreds.

## Slide 12: Business Model
**B2B SaaS — practitioners pay, members free**

### Revenue
- 3 tiers: €19 / €29 / €49 (Essentiel → Pro → Cabinet)
- Members use free (invited by practitioner — zero acquisition cost)
- B2C premium upside (future): €3/mo — not in current model

### Go-to-Market
- Start: France (home market, network)
- Then: EU (UK, Germany) — same language, similar regulations
- Later: N. America — only after proving EU PMF

### Key Metrics (Modeled)
- €19-49 per practitioner/month
- ~83% modeled gross margin (unproven)
- <12mo modeled CAC payback
