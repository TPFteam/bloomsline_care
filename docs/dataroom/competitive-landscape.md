## Competitor Profiles

### 1. SimplePractice (Practice Management)
- **One-liner:** US-focused EHR for solo mental health practitioners
- **Pricing:** $49–99/mo
- **Target:** US solo/small-group therapists
- **Scale:** 250K+ practitioners, owned by Vista Equity Partners ($4B acquisition)
- **Differentiator:** All-in-one billing, scheduling, notes, and telehealth
- **Moat:** Deep US insurance integrations & network effects among therapists
- **Strengths:** Best-in-class EHR for US solo therapists; Strong brand — 250K+ practitioners; Comprehensive billing & insurance
- **Weaknesses:** No member-facing app or engagement tools; Zero AI in the care journey; US-only — no EU presence
- **Threat Level:** Medium — Scale and brand could enable EU expansion
- **Defense:** They have no member app, no AI, and no EU presence. Rebuilding for B2B2C would require a full product rethink.
- **Features:** Practitioner Tools ✅ | Member App ❌ | AI Companion ❌ | European ❌ | B2B2C ❌

### 2. Jane App (Practice Management)
- **One-liner:** Canadian practice management for health & wellness clinics
- **Pricing:** CAD $54–99/mo
- **Target:** Canadian/AU/UK allied health & wellness clinics
- **Scale:** Mostly bootstrapped, 200K+ practitioners, $1.8B valuation (2025)
- **Differentiator:** Multi-discipline support (physio, chiro, massage, mental health)
- **Moat:** Broad allied health coverage & strong Canadian base
- **Strengths:** Multi-discipline flexibility; Strong in Canada/Australia/UK — 50K+ clinics; Profitable since day one, ~$100M ARR
- **Weaknesses:** Limited mental health specialization; No AI capabilities; Minimal European footprint
- **Threat Level:** Low — Not mental health focused, no AI or EU strategy
- **Features:** Practitioner Tools ✅ | Member App partial | AI Companion ❌ | European partial | B2B2C ❌

### 3. Doctolib (Practice Management)
- **One-liner:** Dominant European healthcare booking & telehealth platform
- **Pricing:** €139/mo
- **Target:** European GPs, specialists, hospitals
- **Scale:** 80M patients, 400K practitioners, ~$850M raised, valued at $6.4B
- **Differentiator:** Largest EU patient booking network with 80M+ users
- **Moat:** Massive network effects — 80M patients, deep health system integrations
- **Strengths:** Dominant EU booking platform; Massive user base and trust; €348M ARR (2024), approaching profitability
- **Weaknesses:** Booking + admin only — no care features; No AI companion or between-session tools; Not specialized for mental health
- **Threat Level:** High — 80M users in EU — could add care features at any time
- **Defense:** Doctolib is a booking engine, not a care platform. Adding AI-powered between-session care would require a completely different product DNA.
- **Features:** Practitioner Tools partial | Member App partial | AI Companion ❌ | European ✅ | B2B2C ❌

### 4. Headspace (B2C Wellness)
- **One-liner:** Market-leading meditation & mindfulness content app
- **Pricing:** $12.99/mo
- **Target:** Consumers seeking mindfulness & meditation
- **Scale:** 80M+ downloads, merged with Ginger → Headspace Health ($3B valuation)
- **Differentiator:** Premium brand and library of meditation/sleep content
- **Strengths:** Massive brand awareness — 80M+ downloads; Rich content library; B2B enterprise offering
- **Weaknesses:** Content only — no practitioner connection; No personalized AI or care plans; Generic wellness, not clinical mental health
- **Threat Level:** Low — Content play — different product category entirely
- **Features:** Practitioner Tools ❌ | Member App ✅ | AI Companion ❌ | European ✅ | B2B2C ❌

### 5. BetterHelp (B2C Wellness)
- **One-liner:** Largest online therapy marketplace — text, phone & video
- **Pricing:** $280–400/mo
- **Target:** US consumers seeking affordable online therapy
- **Scale:** 5M+ users served, Teladoc subsidiary ($1B+ annual revenue)
- **Differentiator:** Largest online therapy marketplace by volume
- **Strengths:** Massive scale — 5M+ users, 30K+ therapists; Strong marketing & SEO machine; Low friction onboarding
- **Weaknesses:** No practitioner tools — therapists are commoditized; No between-session engagement; Revenue declining 8% YoY (2024), US-only
- **Threat Level:** Low — Marketplace model misaligned with care continuity
- **Defense:** BetterHelp commoditizes therapists. Practitioners don't choose it — they tolerate it. We empower practitioners, not replace them.
- **Features:** Practitioner Tools ❌ | Member App ✅ | AI Companion ❌ | European ❌ | B2B2C ❌

### 6. Spring Health (Enterprise B2B)
- **One-liner:** Enterprise mental health platform with care matching
- **Pricing:** ~$5–14 PEPM
- **Target:** Large employers (1,000+ employees)
- **Scale:** $509M raised, valued at $3.3B (Series E, Jul 2024)
- **Differentiator:** AI-powered care navigation for enterprise employees
- **Strengths:** Strong enterprise sales — 450 employers, 20M+ lives covered; AI-powered care matching; $509M in funding, 3,400 employees
- **Weaknesses:** No practitioner SaaS tools; US-focused, limited EU presence; Employees only — no independent practitioner market
- **Threat Level:** Medium — Resources to expand into EU or add practitioner tools
- **Defense:** Spring Health sells to HR departments, not practitioners. Different buyer, different product, different market.
- **Features:** Practitioner Tools ❌ | Member App partial | AI Companion ❌ | European ❌ | B2B2C ❌

### 7. Moka.care (Enterprise B2B)

- **Target:** French/EU mid-size to large companies

- **Threat Level:** Low — B2B-only model, no practitioner SaaS or AI
- **Defense:** Moka.care serves companies, not practitioners. Different buyer, different value prop. No overlap with our B2B2C model.
- **Features:** Practitioner Tools ❌ | Member App partial | AI Companion ❌ | European ✅ | B2B2C ❌

### 8. Woebot (AI Mental Health)
- **One-liner:** CBT-based AI chatbot — consumer app shut down June 2025
- **Pricing:** B2B only (after B2C shutdown)
- **Target:** Enterprise clients (after B2C shutdown)
- **Scale:** $123M raised, Stanford-backed, B2C app shut down June 2025
- **Differentiator:** Most clinically validated AI mental health chatbot
- **Strengths:** Strongest clinical evidence in AI mental health; Stanford/research credibility; FDA Breakthrough Device designation (May 2021)
- **Weaknesses:** B2C model failed — app shut down June 2025; No practitioner tools; Pivoted to enterprise-only (limited TAM)
- **Threat Level:** Low — B2C failure validates our care-anchored approach
- **Defense:** Woebot's shutdown proves standalone AI mental health doesn't work. Our AI is embedded in a real care relationship — the model they couldn't build.
- **Features:** Practitioner Tools ❌ | Member App ❌ | AI Companion ✅ | European ❌ | B2B2C ❌

### 9. Wysa (AI Mental Health)
- **One-liner:** AI mental health chatbot with CBT/DBT exercises

- **Threat Level:** Low — Standalone AI chatbot — same limitations as Woebot
- **Defense:** Wysa is a self-help tool with no practitioner connection. Our AI knows the member's care plan, milestones, and practitioner — contextual, not generic.
- **Features:** Practitioner Tools ❌ | Member App ✅ | AI Companion ✅ | European ❌ | B2B2C ❌

## Bloomsline Differentiators

1. **B2B2C Model** — Target: €50 to acquire 1 practitioner → ~12 members invited for free. Effective member CAC: ~€4 vs €30-50 for B2C apps.
2. **AI in the Care Journey** — Bloom knows the member's practitioner, milestones, and session history. Embedded in a real care relationship.
3. **Both Sides Connected** — No major competitor connects both sides today. SimplePractice has no member app. BetterHelp has no practitioner tools.
4. **European White Space** — SimplePractice, BetterHelp, Spring Health — all US-only. Doctolib dominates booking but is not a care platform.
5. **Price as a Weapon** — €19-49/mo vs €50-139/mo competitors. 85%+ target gross margin with AI optimized on Claude Haiku.

---

## Uncontested Market Spaces

1. **Between-Session Care** — The 167 hours between weekly sessions are completely unserved. 20-50% of therapy homework goes uncompleted.
2. **AI Anchored in Care Relationships** — Woebot proved standalone AI fails. Market needs AI embedded in real therapeutic relationships.
3. **European Practitioner SaaS** — No modern mental health SaaS operates in Europe. EU mental health apps market: $2.2B (2025), projected $4.8B by 2030.
4. **Dual-Sided B2B2C Platform** — Every competitor is one-sided. Nobody connects both in a single ecosystem.
5. **Affordable AI-Native Practice Tool** — Practice management tools cost €50-139/mo. Bloomsline starts at €19/mo with AI + member app.

## Strategic Recommendations

1. **(Critical)** Own "between-session care" as a category — No competitor occupies this space.
2. **(Critical)** Win France before expanding — 100-200 French practitioners first, then DE/ES/UK.
3. **(Important)** Use B2B2C as the moat — 1 practitioner sale → ~12 members, organic distribution.
4. **(Opportunity)** Position for the "Woebot gap" — AI anchored in real care, not standalone.
