## Key Research Findings

### The Between-Session Gap
- Half of every session is spent just catching up on what happened during the week
- Practitioners have zero visibility into what happens between visits
- Members feel alone between sessions and often disengage from the therapeutic process

### From Doctalink to Bloomsline
Our first product (Doctalink) was a platform for practitioner profiles — an online directory/visibility tool. Through extensive research we discovered:
- The practitioner profile/directory market was **crowded** (SimplePractice, Jane, Zocdoc, Doctolib already dominate)
- The timing was **early** for a standalone directory play
- The **real gap** everyone described was what happens between sessions
- Neither practice management tools nor wellness apps address this gap

### What Practitioners Told Us
- They want to see their client's week, not just hear about it in session
- They spend too much time "catching up" and not enough going deep
- They need tools that respect the therapeutic relationship, not replace it
- They want to assign resources/worksheets between sessions but lack good tools

### What Members/Users Told Us
- They want to feel connected to their practitioner between sessions
- They struggle to remember what happened during the week when session starts
- Journaling apps feel generic — they want something connected to their care
- They'd use an AI companion if it felt safe and connected to their practitioner

## Research-Driven Product Decisions

| Insight | Product Decision |
|---------|-----------------|
| Session catch-up wastes time | Practitioner dashboard shows week summary |
| Members forget what happened | Moment capture (10-sec photo/voice/text) |
| Generic apps feel disconnected | Everything connects back to the practitioner |
| Practitioners want worksheets | One-click worksheet creation and sharing |
| Members want AI but safe | Bloom AI companion connected to care context |
| No streaks/guilt in mental health | Gentle UX philosophy (GUX) |

## Competitive Discovery

Through research, we mapped the existing landscape into two buckets — and found nobody in between:

**Practice Tools** (SimplePractice, Jane, TherapyNotes)
- Run the business: billing, scheduling, clinical notes
- Don't address between-session engagement

**Wellness Apps** (Headspace, Calm, BetterHelp)
- Help individuals cope: meditations, self-help, generic content
- Not connected to a specific practitioner or care plan

**The Gap:** Who connects the practitioner and the client between sessions?
**The Answer:** Bloomsline.
