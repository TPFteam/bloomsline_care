# Founding Practitioner Agreement

**Bloomsline — First 10 Program**

---

## The Offer

Bloomsline is selecting **10 founding practitioners** to shape the future of the platform. In exchange for your early commitment and ongoing feedback, you receive a deal that will never be offered again.

---

## Terms

### What You Get

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **6 months free access** | Full platform access at no cost from the day you onboard |
| **Locked price: €22/month + applicable taxes** | After the free period, your price is locked at €22/month for **3 years** — regardless of future price increases or new features added |
| **All features included** | Every feature we ship — current and future — is included in your plan for the duration of the price lock |
| **Founding Practitioner badge** | Visible on your Bloomsline profile and public story page |
| **Direct input** | Priority access to the founding team for feedback, feature requests, and product direction |

### What We Ask

| Commitment | Details |
|------------|---------|
| **Weekly check-in** | A short (~15 min) weekly call or async feedback during the first 3 months to help us improve the product |
| **Onboard at least 3 clients** | Invite a minimum of 3 clients to use the member app within the first 2 months |
| **Honest feedback** | Tell us what works, what doesn't, and what's missing — we build for you |

---

## Pricing Breakdown

| Period | Price |
|--------|-------|
| **Months 1-6** | Free (€0) |
| **Months 7 onwards** (up to 3 years from signing) | €22/month + applicable taxes |
| **After year 3** | Standard pricing at the time (currently projected €29-49/month), with 30 days notice of any change |

- Billing starts automatically at month 7 unless you cancel
- **You can cancel anytime** — no lock-in, no penalties, no questions
- If you cancel during the free period, you owe nothing
- If you cancel after month 6, your subscription ends at the current billing cycle

---

## Why €22/month

The standard price for Bloomsline will be higher. We're offering €22/month to founding practitioners because:

- You're taking a bet on us before the product is finished
- Your feedback directly shapes what we build
- We want practitioners who believe in this mission, not just users

This price is exclusively for the **first 10 practitioners** who sign this agreement.

3. I understand this is a **cancellable subscription** — I can leave at any time with no penalty
