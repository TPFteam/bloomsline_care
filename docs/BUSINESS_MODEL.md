# Bloomsline — Business Model

## Model Type

**B2B SaaS** — Practitioners pay, members use free.

---

## Revenue Model

| Revenue Stream | Description |
|---------------|-------------|
| **Practitioner Subscription** | Monthly SaaS per seat |
| **Members Use Free** | Invited by their practitioner — zero friction onboarding |
| **Clinic/Enterprise Tiers** | Volume pricing for group practices and organizations |

## Pricing

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Price per practitioner** | €29-79/month |
| **Target gross margin** | 90%+ |

---

## Why B2B, Not B2C

- **Lower CAC:** One sale to a practitioner activates 20-50 members
- **Built-in distribution:** Practitioners invite their own clients
- **Higher retention:** Practitioner is the anchor — if they use it, members follow
- **Regulatory fit:** B2B model aligns with healthcare procurement and compliance
- **Revenue predictability:** SaaS subscription vs. consumer whims

---

## Go-to-Market Strategy

### Phase 1: Start in Europe
- **Markets:** France, UK, Germany
- **Why:** Strong mental health infrastructure, regulatory support, founder network
- **Approach:** Direct practitioner outreach, PLG (Practitioner-Led Growth)

### Phase 2: Expand Globally
- **Markets:** North America, APAC
- **Why:** Largest TAM, Medicare digital health coverage, high digital adoption

### Phase 3: Partnerships
- **EHR integrations:** Connect with existing practice management tools
- **Employer programs:** B2B2B — sell to employers who offer it to their employee wellness programs

## Growth Flywheel

```
Practitioner joins
    ↓
Invites 20-50 members
    ↓
Members engage between sessions
    ↓
Members see OTHER practitioners
    ↓
Those practitioners hear about Bloomsline
    ↓
New practitioner joins
    ↓
(Repeat — Care Network Effect)
```
