# Bloomsline — Project Overview

## What is Bloomsline?

Bloomsline is a **between-session care platform** that connects practitioners and members through gentle, consistent growth. It fills the gap between therapy sessions — the 167 hours per week where people are on their own.

## One-liner

> Where small moments become meaningful change.

## The Two Sides

### For Members (Mobile App — Expo/React Native)
- Capture moments in 10 seconds — photo, voice, or text
- AI-discovered patterns in mood and behavior
- Bloom — an always-available AI companion
- Daily rituals that adapt to energy levels
- **Outcome:** They stay engaged between sessions.

### For Practitioners (Web Dashboard — Next.js)
- See their client's week through what they share
- Create & share worksheets in one click
- Start sessions already informed
- Track client engagement over time
- **Outcome:** Sessions go deeper, faster.

## Core Tagline

> One platform. Two sides of care. Connected.

## The Problem We Solve

Therapy is 1 hour a week. Life is the other 167. That's where people need support — and where progress gets lost.

**Three consequences:**
1. Half of every session is spent just catching up
2. Practitioners can't see what happens between visits
3. People feel alone between sessions — and disengage

## Origin Story

We first built **Doctalink** — a platform for practitioner profiles. The market was crowded and timing was early. But through 68 practitioner interviews across 7 countries and 119 user interviews, we discovered the real gap: **what happens between sessions**. That insight became Bloomsline.

> "We didn't just build a product. We earned the insight to build the right one."

## Vision

1. **Phase 1:** Therapeutic support platform
2. **Phase 2:** Behavioral data labeling
3. **Phase 3:** Human intelligence research lab

> Beyond an app. A research lab for humanity.
