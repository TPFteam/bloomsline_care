# Bloomsline — Founding Practitioner Agreement

**Practitioner:** ____________________
**Position:** Founding Practitioner #__ of 10
**Start date:** ____________________

You are one of the first ten practitioners on Bloomsline. This agreement sets out what you receive in exchange for building the platform with us.

## 1. Lifetime price lock
You pay **€__/month** (the rate of your current tier) for as long as you remain an active subscriber. We will never raise this rate. If we lower public prices, you get the lower rate. The lock applies to your tier at signing; upgrades to a higher tier are at that tier's then-current public price.

## 2. Founding Practitioner status
- "Practicien Fondateur" badge on your Bloomsline profile.
- Recognition in our website footer ("Built with these 10 practitioners") and in marketing materials.
- Seat on the Customer Advisory Board: monthly 30-min product call, votes on roadmap priorities.
- Direct WhatsApp line to the founder for 12 months.

## 3. Six free months
Your first six months are at €0. Any subscription fees already paid are credited as service balance.

## 4. Trainee seats
Up to **three free seats** for supervisees, students, or stagiaires under your supervision. Seats convert to standard paid pricing only if/when the trainee leaves your supervision.

## 5. Marketplace priority
When Bloomsline launches its public B2C marketplace, you receive top-position listing in your specialty and city, **for life, at no additional cost**.

## 6. Referral rewards
For each practitioner you refer who reaches 3 active months on Bloomsline:
- **+12 months of free subscription** added to your account (stackable, no cap).
- The 3rd successful referral triggers **"Co-Founder" status**: paid attendance at one AFTCC or FFPP conference per year (up to €600), branded materials, and a published case study on bloomsline.com.

Referral rewards are paid as service credits, not cash, to remain within French health-professional ethics rules (compérage).

## 7. Founding Bonus Right
You hold a contractual right to a one-time cash bonus on a Bloomsline liquidity event (sale of the company, share transfer exceeding 50% of capital, or IPO):

- **0.05% of the company's exit valuation, capped at €25,000.**
- Vests linearly over 4 years from your start date (25% per year).
- Paid out only if you are an active Bloomsline subscriber at the time of the event.
- Across all 10 Founding Practitioners, total bonus pool is capped at **€250,000 / 0.5% of exit value, whichever is lower**.

**This is a contractual cash bonus, not a share of Bloomsline SAS.** You are not a shareholder, you have no voting rights, you incur no shareholder risk, and you receive no information rights or financial disclosures. The bonus is treated as ordinary compensation income; French income tax and social charges apply at receipt.

## 8. Your commitments
In exchange for the above, you agree to:
- **Feedback:** attend at least **8 of 12** monthly Advisory Board calls per year.
- **Case study:** allow one anonymized written case study **and** one short video testimonial per year (using your name and likeness; no patient data, no identifiable client information).
- **Honest defects:** report bugs and product friction in writing, even when inconvenient.

## 9. Exit and re-entry
- You may cancel your Bloomsline subscription at any time, no penalty.
- If you cancel:
  - Lifetime price lock and trainee seats pause.
  - Re-subscription within 12 months → all benefits resume, vesting continues uninterrupted.
  - Re-subscription after 12 months → you re-enter at current public pricing; the **unvested** portion of your Founding Bonus Right lapses; vested portion remains payable on a liquidity event.
- If Bloomsline terminates this agreement for cause (fraud, professional misconduct, bringing the platform into disrepute) all unvested benefits lapse.

## 10. Program limits
This agreement applies to a maximum of **10 practitioners total**. No Founding Practitioner agreements will be issued after the tenth is countersigned. Bloomsline reserves the right to close the program earlier.

## 11. Governing law
Governed by French law. Disputes are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Tribunal de Commerce de Paris.
