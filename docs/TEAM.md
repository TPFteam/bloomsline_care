## Founding Story

Started as **Doctalink** — a practitioner profile/directory platform. Through 68 practitioner interviews across 7 countries and 119 user interviews, discovered the market was crowded and timing early. But the research revealed the real opportunity: **between-session care**. Pivoted to Bloomsline in Q3 2025. MVP completed by December 2025. Currently finding product-market fit (Q1 2026).
